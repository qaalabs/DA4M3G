Here are some progressive tasks organised by difficulty - learners read the task and write the query themselves:

Level 1 - Exploration

How many users are in the database?
How many products are in the database?
Show the first 20 products - what columns are available?
How many different order statuses are there and what are they?
Show all users from Germany


Level 2 - Filtering and sorting
6. Show all products in the Jeans category sorted by retail price from highest to lowest
7. Show all orders that have been returned
8. Show the 10 most expensive products
9. Show all female users who joined after 2020
10. Show all order items with a sale price greater than 100

Level 3 - Aggregations
11. How many orders has each user made? Show the top 20
12. What is the total revenue (sale price) by product category?
13. What is the average sale price per order status?
14. Which country has the most users?
15. How many products does each brand have? Show only brands with more than 10 products

Level 4 - Joins
16. Show all orders with the first name and last name of the customer
17. Show all order items with the product name and category
18. Which users have never placed an order? (hint - LEFT JOIN)
19. Show the top 10 customers by total amount spent
20. Show all orders with the customer name, product name, and sale price

Level 5 - Stretch and challenge
21. What is the most popular product category by number of items sold per country?
22. What percentage of orders have been returned?
23. Show the month-by-month revenue trend
24. Which distribution centre supplies the most products?
25. Create a view that shows all completed orders with customer and product details
