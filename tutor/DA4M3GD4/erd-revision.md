## ERD-REVISION: Table Design Scenarios (revision)

Lab: docs/day4/erd-revision.md

- Revises Day 1's ~02-ERD~ theory block - a quick recap of PK/FK and cardinality notation is worth doing first if it's been a few days
- Four independent scenarios (Library, Restaurant, Vehicle Hire, Conference) - assign one per group; drop a scenario if fewer groups, double one up if more
- Groups identify entities, attributes, PK/FK, and relationship cardinality - suggested table names are a hint, not a full answer
- Watch for the usual slips: a many-to-many left as two 1:M lines with no junction table, missing FK on the "many" side, an attribute that's really its own entity
- Debrief: pick one group per scenario to justify a relationship's cardinality to the room, not just read out the table list
