## ERD Revision - Approach

### Exercise 1: Review and plan client requirements
1. Work out which tables would be required in the database.
2. Draw a diagram to specify which columns should go into which tables.
3. Using the diagram, plan your foreign keys.

*Share your diagram with the group*

### Exercise 2: Refine the column names
1. Review your diagram.
2. Decide if the column names are satisfactory or need refining.
3. Make any required changes to your diagram.
